OUR PURITY PROMISE

How We Make India's
Purest Malai Ice Cream

🥛

100% Pure A2 Desi Gir Cow Milk

Sourced directly from free-grazing indigenous Gir cows in Gujarat. Naturally rich in A2 protein and thick golden cream, with zero palm oil, dalda, or artificial thickeners.

🍨

Traditional Slow Malai Churning

We slowly reduce full-cream malai to thick rabri consistency before slow-churning in sub-zero barrels. That gives you dense, velvety scoops with zero icy crystals and no excess air.

🥭

Real Regional Ingredients from Across India

Real GI-tagged Kashmiri Kesar from Pampore, Devgad & Ratnagiri Alphonso mangoes, fragrant green cardamom from Coorg, and natural wild forest honey.
