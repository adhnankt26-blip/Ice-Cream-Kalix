THE VEDIC CRAFT

The Craft of Indian
Slow-Folded Ice Cream

🪬

100% Single-Herd A2 Gir Cow Milk

Sourced exclusively from ethically nurtured, free-grazing Gir cows in Gujarat. Rich in A2 beta-casein proteins and golden butterfat (18%+) with zero synthetic gums.

🔔

72-Hour Slow Malai Churn in Brass

We slow-reduce full-cream malai for 36 hours before transferring to sub-zero bell-brass kulfi churn barrels, yielding silk-sheet density.

🌿

Direct-Origin Terroirs Across India

GI-tagged saffron from Pampore, Alphonso mangoes from Devgad & Ratnagiri, mountain cardamom from Coorg, and wild forest honey from the Nilgiris.
